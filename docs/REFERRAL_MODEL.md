# Proposed member referral rewards

**Invite 10 new paying members and enjoy the following month free. Bring 120 and earn 12 free months in total.**

Every member receives a personal invitation link. When someone joins through that link and becomes a qualifying paid member, the inviter earns one referral credit. The new member can then invite their own contacts and earn rewards through their own direct invitations.

| Total qualifying direct referrals | Total free Pro access earned |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | 1 month |
| 20 | 2 months |
| 60 | 6 months |
| 120 | 1 year |

Credits accumulate over time; they do not reset each month. Each block of 10 earns one future month, and the 120-member milestone represents a year's total reward. It does not grant an extra year on top of the monthly rewards already earned.

The first earned reward starts on the first day of the following calendar month. Further completed groups of 10 add successive free calendar months to the member's reward queue. For example, 20 qualifying referrals in October earn November and December free; 7 referrals remain progress toward the next group of 10. Members receive free future access, with no cash payout or refund of membership already purchased.

## Recommended qualification rules

A referral qualifies after a new customer's first successful paid purchase. There is no refund-window waiting requirement. Free registrations can appear as pending referrals. Duplicate accounts, self-referrals, failed payments, and repeated renewals do not add credits. Attribute each customer to one inviter, fixed at signup; do not pay rewards for downstream invitations.

Rewards are free software access. There are no cash commissions or charges to participate in the referral program. Members should invite people who would benefit from the quoting product and clearly disclose their referral benefit when promoting it.

The member dashboard should show the invite link, pending and qualified counts, progress to the next 10-member milestone, the next free month, and the queue of future rewarded months. Publish the attribution and abuse-review rules before launch.

## Status

This document articulates the requested system. Referral links, attribution, tracking, and automated rewards are **not implemented** in the current app. Existing paid access remains ₺399 for 30 days through iyzico. Implementing referral rewards would require additional database records, payment-confirmation attribution, future calendar-month entitlements, and member dashboard screens. The proposed referral reward is free future access; this document does not set a legal no-refund policy for purchases.
